# Student Monthly Budget Analyzer

A lightweight and efficient Python application designed specifically for students to monitor their finances, categorize expenses, and gain better control over their monthly budgets.

## 🚀 Features

* **Income & Expense Tracking:** Easily record your monthly allowance, earnings, and everyday expenses.
* **Categorized Breakdown:** Group expenses into practical student categories (e.g., Tuition, Rent, Food, Textbooks, Entertainment).
* **Budget Analysis:** Automatically calculate total spending, remaining balance, and savings percentage.
* **Simple CLI Interface:** Clean, text-based prompts that make tracking finances quick and straightforward.
